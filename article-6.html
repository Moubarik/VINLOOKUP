Finding and Verifying the VIN

Look through the lower driver's side of the windshield, check the driver's door jamb label, and compare both to the title and registration. Insurance cards sometimes carry the same number. Every location must agree. A single mismatched character means stop.

Free Data Layers

NHTSA: free decoder + open recall status. NICB: free check against participating insurers for stolen unrecovered and insurance-reported salvage/flood. Independent free VIN lookup tools: available title, accident, or auction signals. Paid NMVTIS providers: deeper official title-brand and odometer history when needed.

What Success Looks Like

You reject three cars in ten minutes because free data already shows salvage brands or declining mileage. You then spend money on one commercial report and one inspection for the single remaining candidate. Total research cost stays low while risk drops.

FAQ Essentials

Free VIN tools exist and require no payment. They are not complete substitutes for Carfax. Some need no sign-up. A clean free result does not prove the car is problem-free. Always inspect.

Start with the free VIN lookup. Keep paid reports and the mechanic for the car you actually intend to own.
